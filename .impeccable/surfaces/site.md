# Surface: public site (domios.pt)

Mode: Persuade (home), Read (guide and per-menu pages). Separate Vite entry in domios-frontend, own deploy. Languages: pt-PT and en.

Audience and job: a Portuguese household that does not have an account. They must understand what DomiOS is in seconds and ask for access (invite-only product). Primary action: "Pedir acesso" (form on its own page, button on every page). Proof: real app captures (local test data, labelled as example data) and the one real mechanism: price verdict against 90-day history and Omnibus check. No invented prices, savings, counts or testimonials; every number on screen is an example, labelled.

Unresolved: final copy review by the user; real captures replace placeholders once recorded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one readable shelf label: the product's own price-verdict mechanism, shown large and live, instead of a headline over a screenshot. Refuses the split hero with device mockup and the row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: DomiOS "Aisle Sign" system, palette fixed (cool paper, white label surface, teal "good now", navy structure, six aisle hues only where a category appears). Schibsted Grotesk, 800 only for prices and wordmark, 1px hairlines, 8/12/16px corners, flat, no shadows. Shelf label, verdict foot row with 8px dot, aisle dot, pill chips.

STORY: Visitor reads a shelf label that judges its own price, understands DomiOS tells you if a price is good against history, sees each menu (catalog, promotions, recipes, plan, pantry, lists, house) as another label that opens its page, and requests access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, display headline and one-line lede with the primary button "Pedir acesso" and a secondary text link "Ver como funciona". Right, a large interactive shelf label (example product, example price in 800 at price-hero size, four verdict chips below it to flip between historic low, below average, usual, above average; the label's verdict foot and 90-day range bar change). Labelled "Exemplo". Top bar: wordmark, menu links, language switch, "Pedir acesso" button.

FORM: Shelf-label demonstrator, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, dealt lead of the roll. seed: 43745a0c7d5374d5
